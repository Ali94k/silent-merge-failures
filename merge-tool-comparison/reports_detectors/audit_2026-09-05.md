Audited 2026-09-05. Targets: `FINDINGS.md` (sections 1–11), `adjudication/sd6_rulings.csv` (22 rows, 20 units),

## Verdict

*Ruling applied 2026-09-05 (Ali): one unit per distinct merge content and the design-spec twin excluded. Applied strictly, the counting rule also removes the clean `3de930e0ed` duplicate, so the citable population is 107 (the sensitivity table below, written before the ruling, shows 108 for the two-exclusion view; the family cells are identical).*

**Every ruling holds. The population does not.** All 15 ImportPruneUsage CAUSAL units, the 5 baseline+experimental
CAUSAL units and both DETECTOR-FP rulings survive re-derivation on the merged source (repos are on disk, so same-package
resolution could be excluded by `git ls-tree` at the merge commit). Every headline digit in FINDINGS §1–§11 reproduces.
What does not hold is the claim that the held-out split is held out, and the claim that the ruling round was an
independent check: one of the 16 family catches is byte-identical to a D1 design-spec unit, another is the same merge
counted twice, and all 22 committed rationales are the assistant's drafts accepted with one key. The point estimate is
robust to every correction (see the table below); the *description* of what was measured is not.

denominators only.

inflates n=110 by one.

### H2. The rationale record is 22/22 accepted drafts, and 3 of the 14 D1 rationales contradict the artifacts
`acceptDraft()` writes `"[accepted Claude draft] " + draft_why` and sets every lane; all 22 committed rows carry that
prefix; 21/22 are byte-identical to the current draft text; 0/20 unit overrides; the rationale textarea was never used.
FINDINGS L15–16 cites the file as "Ali's GD4 rulings … written rationales". Three rationales assert things the sources
contradict while the rulings still hold:
- `apache_commons-collections`: "surviving `PairedIterator.PairedItem` nested import does not import the enclosing
  name (JLS 7.5.1)" — no such import exists in any version of `IteratorUtilsTest.java`; it is in the sibling
  `IterableUtils.java`. The real mechanism (pruned `iterators.*` wildcard, bare `PairedIterator` at L1124) holds.
- `google_truth`: "merged has 15 bare `fail(...)` calls" — merged has **one** bare `fail(` (L64); 4 are `ASSERT.fail(`,
  the rest are the token in string literals. One uncovered static call is still a compile error.
- `steveice10_mcprotocollib`: the committed text is the **pre-refinement** draft ("all three verified uncovered");
  commit `e62f7cc` (10:14) had already retracted the `MetadataType.java` sub-flag as a same-package FP, 16 minutes
  before the CSV commit `93ed640` (10:30). FINDINGS §3 reports the refined fact; the ruling record contradicts it.

### H3. The pre-filled-draft mechanism is disclosed only for the baseline
After the Stage-C audit, ch.5 §5.3.5, ch.8 §8.4.2/§8.5 and ch.11 §11.5 now say the *baseline's* rulings were made
against pre-filled draft labels with a one-key accept, every draft accepted, and that a re-read changed 2 of 11. The
held-out round used the same page design ("per the Stage-C §4.6 DRAFTS pattern" in the generator's own comment),
with the unit's category, family membership, stratum and pass labels visible, 20/20 accepted — and no chapter says
so. Ch.8 §8.4.5 reads "I ruled every flagged held-out unit, each with a written rationale"; the record supports
"accepted the assistant's rationale".

### H4. "Draft-acceptance 20/20 mirrors the #29/#30 precedent" is offered as reassurance
FINDINGS §9 L183–185 and THREATS §7 L498–501. The #29 precedent is the Stage-C round whose identical procedure produced
2 wrong CAUSAL rulings of 11; THREATS row 14 in the same file already records that. "Machine-verified drafts" is also
overstated: `apply_rulings.py` checks completeness and coverage, not content.

### H5. D1 has an undisclosed structural ceiling, and it produced D1's only own-category miss
`import_prune_usage.py` `covers()` (L306–309): any surviving non-static wildcard import returns True for a type,
regardless of its package — a stray `import java.util.*;` silences every pruned-import candidate in the file. FINDINGS §5
"Honest ceilings" lists D2's and D3's ceilings and nothing for D1. The audit attributes the one own-category miss
(`jenkinsci_ldap-plugin__3eee59e25a`: theirs pruned `org.kohsuke.accmod.Restricted`/`NoExternalUse`, ours added 30
`@Restricted(NoExternalUse.class)` uses) to exactly this guard; I confirmed the code, not the unit.

### H6. The recall is presented without its denominator
Every chapter that cites 43.2% (ch.1 L107, ch.10 L17, spine L45, ch.8 §8.4.5 and RQ4.3) gives "5.4% → 43.2%" with
neither "of 37 units" nor the whole-held-out rate 18/110 = 16.4%; both appear only inside TODO-LEDGER brackets. Ch.8
§8.4.5 pairs "110 held-out units" with "43.2%" in one sentence. The ledger row `HeldOutFamilyRecall` carries no
denominator. Likewise the suite's held-out precision, 18/20 units = 90.0% [69.9, 97.2], is never stated as a rate;
what travels is D1's 14/14 and the control-side zero.

## Decisions for Ali

1. **Counting rule.** Count one unit per distinct merge content (drop the duplicate b515bac1b0 copy): 15/36, D1 13/13,
   own-category 12/13. This is what "unit = merge" already promises.
2. **The spec twin.** Either exclude `sandergielisse_enderstone__9d0d7b7ba8` from the citable numerator (14/35 = 40.0%)
   or keep it and state in FINDINGS §1, §9 and ch.8 §8.4.5 that one family catch is content-identical to a D1 design-spec
   unit. Excluding is the cleaner story; keeping with disclosure is defensible because the plan's firewall was by id.
3. **The rationale record.** Amend the three contradicted rationales in place (keep rulings), label the file as accepted
   drafts with a dated re-check, and stop describing it as "written rationales" of the rater.
4. **Disclosure parity with the baseline.** Extend the ch.5/ch.8/ch.11 sentences about pre-filled drafts and one-key
   accept to this round (20/20), and replace "mirrors the precedent" in FINDINGS §9 and THREATS §7.
5. **D1 ceiling** into FINDINGS §5 and ch.8 §8.4.8; **denominator** (of 37; whole-held-out 16.4%) and **held-out
   precision** (18/20 = 90%) into the prose that carries 43.2%.
